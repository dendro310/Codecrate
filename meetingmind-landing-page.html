FAQ

Questions before you start

Will prospects see a bot join my call?

No. MeetingMind joins using your name and profile photo. To your prospect, it looks like any other participant. You can also set it to record-only mode with no visible join.

Is my call data secure?

Yes. MeetingMind is SOC-2 Type II compliant. Your recordings are encrypted and stored securely. You own your data — we never use it to train AI models or share it with anyone.

Do I need to tell prospects they're being recorded?

MeetingMind can display a recording notice at the start of each call, depending on your settings. We recommend keeping it on — most prospects expect it, and transparency builds trust.

Does it work with my specific Salesforce fields?

It works with standard and custom fields. During setup, you map which fields MeetingMind should populate (Budget, Decision Maker, Next Steps, or any custom field your team uses). Takes about 5 minutes.

What if it gets something wrong?

MeetingMind is accurate on 95%+ of extracted fields. You can review before syncing if you prefer, or turn on auto-sync and make corrections anytime. It never overwrites your manual notes.

How long does setup take?

Connect your calendar in 60 seconds. Map your CRM fields in 5 minutes. Most teams are running on their first call the same day.
